EditorURLResolverShim.cs omitted: it starts an external resolver and disables certificate checks. Playlist entries must be individual playable URLs. The user's YouTube playlist will be expanded into an ordered snapshot of video URLs before upload; a playlist-page URL alone does not implement sequential playlist playback.
